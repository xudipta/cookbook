# The Layer Cake

A story that teaches Docker with a bakery. Three short chapters: a first layered cake (images, layers and the
cache), re-baking only what changed (the order of Dockerfile lines), and shipping the cake without the kitchen
(multi-stage builds). Play it on a phone: every step is a tap.

<div id="story" data-story="docker">
  <noscript>This story needs JavaScript.</noscript>
</div>

This story runs on the same build simulator as the practice game, so the timings, the cache and the errors are
the real thing. For typing Dockerfiles yourself, play [Layer Cake](../../games/docker/index.md), or read the
[Docker notes](../../../topics/docker/README.md).
