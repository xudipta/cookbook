# Quiet Hours

A story that teaches monitoring and alerting with a night ward. Three short chapters: reading the monitor (what
kind of vital each metric is, and why a good alert pages rarely), the pager that cried wolf (alert on the slow tail
and on where a disk is heading) and a storm night (grouping alerts, riding out a flap, recording an expensive
query). Play it on a phone: every step is a tap.

<div id="story" data-story="observability">
  <noscript>This story needs JavaScript.</noscript>
</div>

This story runs on the same simulator as the practice game: the charts are the simulated week and night, and every
rule you choose is replayed against the last seven days. To build the rules yourself, play
[Quiet Hours](../../games/observability/index.md), or read the
[observability notes](../../../topics/observability/README.md).
